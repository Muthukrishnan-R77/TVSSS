About TVSSS

A member of the TVS Group, precision engineering since 1994.

The TVS Group

TVS Group

TVS Group was founded in 1911 by Sri T.V. Sundaram Iyengar in Madurai, Tamil Nadu. Today, TVS Group is one of India's largest industrial conglomerates with an annual turnover exceeding ₹20,000 crores. The group spans automotive, manufacturing, financial services and logistics sectors across 60+ countries.

TVS Sensing Solutions Private Limited (TVSSS), established in 1994, is a proud member of this heritage group — bringing the same values of precision, quality and customer commitment to every sensing solution it manufactures.
