About
TVSSS

TVS Sensing Solutions Private Limited is a member of the TVS Group — one of India's largest automotive conglomerates. Founded in 1994, TVSSS designs, develops and manufactures precision sensing solutions, switches, solenoids and electro-mechanical assemblies for global automotive and industrial customers.

With state-of-the-art assembly lines, in-house design and manufacturing facilities, and a culture built on trust, quality and customer satisfaction, TVSSS serves leading global OEMs across two-wheeler, passenger car, commercial vehicle, electric vehicle and industrial segments.
