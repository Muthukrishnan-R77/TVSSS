Key Capabilities

Comprehensive Engineering Portfolio

Every system is engineered in-house with certified mechanical design, electrical safety, and industrial software architecture.

1

Man - Vehicle Collision Prevention

Active microwave and LiDAR sensing zones that detect personnel approaching forklifts or AGVs, automatically triggering vehicle speed reduction and warning flashes.

2

Vehicle - Vehicle Collision Avoidance

Cross-intersection radar and RFID sensors that monitor blind corner approaches, alerting heavy vehicle operators before hazardous intersection entries.

3

STOP Sign & Symbol Floor Projectors

High-intensity industrial LED and laser projectors that beam bright STOP signs, pedestrian crosswalks, and caution symbols directly onto shop floor surfaces.

4

Audible & Visual Alert Systems

Multi-tone buzzer systems coupled with 360-degree synchronized LED strobe towers that activate instantly when hazardous proximity thresholds are breached.

5

High-Speed Machine Vision Inspection

Industrial 2D and 3D vision systems executing surface flaw detection, OCR/barcode reading, dimensional measurement, and assembly completeness checks.

6

Smart Plant Traffic Management

Integrated traffic controllers coordinating automated roll-up doors, AGV right-of-way, and pedestrian gate locks for organized facility circulation.

Target Industries

Industrial Applications

Busy Warehouse Gangways

Preventing blind corner forklift collisions with walking personnel and inventory racks.

AGV / AMR Operational Zones

Visual safety corridors and active safety interlocks around autonomous guided vehicles.

Loading Docks & Dispatch

Dynamic dock lock warnings, pedestrian boundary projection, and truck reversing guidance.
