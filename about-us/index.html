Overview

TVS Sensing Solutions (TVSSS) delivers products and solutions to a gamut of segments including Automotive, Industrial, Consumer Durable and IT, offering quality solutions and application support to renowned clients. TVSSS manufactures products of the highest standards to meet customer requirements through in-house engineering capability.

TVSSS has made its mark as key player delivering end to end solutions of IIOT and Industry 4.0 through an ecosystem of trusted & renowned partners and has made its headway in niche markets by adapting to emerging technologies through partnership approach.

Manufacturing Infrastructure

01

Micro / High Precision Horizontal Molding

Specialized micro-injection molding utilizing high-performance engineering thermoplastics (PBT, PPS, PA66, POM) for precision automotive sensor bodies.

02

High Precision / High Speed Stamping

High-speed progressive stamping presses for critical electrical contact parts in Copper, Brass, Beryllium Copper (BeCu), and Silver alloys.

03

Insert Molding

Integrated insert molding encapsulating high-conductivity terminals, busbars, and lead frames inside rugged automotive housings.

04

Joining Processes

Ultrasonic welding, automated laser micro-welding, and precision resistance welding guaranteeing hermetic seals.

05

Wiring Harness & Connector Assembly

Automated cut, strip, and crimp wiring harness lines with 100% pull-force verification and waterproof connector sealing.

06

Low Pressure Molding & Sealing

Polyamide hot-melt low-pressure molding (LPM) for direct encapsulation of sensitive electronic PCBA sensors.
