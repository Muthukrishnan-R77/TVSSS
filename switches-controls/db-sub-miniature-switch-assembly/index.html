Product Overview

The DB Miniature Switch Assembly is precision-engineered by TVS Sensing Solutions for high-demand automotive and industrial environments. Manufactured under rigorous quality protocols in our IATF 16949-certified facility in Madurai, India, it ensures micro-tolerance accuracy, robust electromagnetic immunity, and maximum operational reliability.

Each unit undergoes comprehensive End-of-Line (EOL) functional validation to guarantee compliance with stringent OEM specifications and extreme thermal-mechanical requirements.
