Key Capabilities

Comprehensive Engineering Portfolio

Every system is engineered in-house with certified mechanical design, electrical safety, and industrial software architecture.

1

Comprehensive Functional Testers

Custom automated fixtures evaluating full operating envelopes, response curves, actuation forces, and sensor output signals under real-world loads.

2

Electrical Safety & Hi-Pot Testing

Precision high-voltage insulation resistance, dielectric withstand, ground bond, and leakage current testing stations compliant with global safety standards.

3

Helium & Air Leakage Testers

Differential pressure decay and high-sensitivity helium mass spectrometry leak detectors ensuring IP67/IP69K sealing on automotive modules.

4

Damper & Actuator Dynamic Testers

Servo-hydraulic and servo-electric dynamic response testing machines measuring force vs. velocity profiles on shock absorbers and actuators.

5

Automated Calibration & Trimming

Laser trimming, magnetic offset tuning, and automated sensor zero-point calibration stations with integrated EEPROM parameter writing.

6

Cloud & MES Serialization Logging

Automatic 2D barcode scanning, serialization linking, real-time Cp/Cpk analytics, and automatic reject bin lockout mechanisms.

Target Industries

Industrial Applications

Automotive Sensors & Switches

100% EOL validation of wheel speed, throttle position, neutral switch, and temperature sensors.

Electric Vehicle Subsystems

High-voltage relay, contactor, and battery disconnect unit (BDU) functional verification.

Industrial Actuators & Solenoids

Pull-in/drop-out voltage, stroke displacement, and holding force dynamic testing.

Hydraulic & Pneumatic Valves

Burst pressure, seat leakage, and flow rate validation test stands.
